   Your live URLs will be:
   • Dashboard: https://store-intelligence.onrender.com

   Steps:

   b) Deploy from: dashboard/app.py
   c) Set API_URL environment variable
   
   Your live URL:
   • Dashboard: https://yourusername-store-intelligence.streamlit.app

   Your live URLs:
   • Dashboard: https://store-intelligence.up.railway.app
   • API: https://store-api.up.railway.app
